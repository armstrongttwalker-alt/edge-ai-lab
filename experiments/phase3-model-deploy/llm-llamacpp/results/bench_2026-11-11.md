Key findings:
- PP512 scales well with threads: 4t=63, 8t=117, 12t=122 tok/s
- TG128 peaks at t=4 (22.78) — adding more threads hurts (memory-bound)
- Optimal: t=4 for TG, t=8~12 for PP
- llama-server /v1/chat/completions verified working, response correct

Comparison with FlagOS (MiniCPM-V 4.7, different model, optimized path):
- FlagOS PP512: ~589 tok/s (Triton/Neon SDOT optimized, same hw)
- llama.cpp PP512: ~117 tok/s (generic ARM path)
- Gap: ~5x — shows potential of further ARM optimization on this platform
